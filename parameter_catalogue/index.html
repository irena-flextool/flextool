    Parameter catalogue

    Every input parameter of this FlexTool version, generated from its database schema: one table per entity class. The topic pages of Model parameters explain what the parameters do and how they work together; the parameter groups page explains the group labels.

    • Unit is the unit of the default convention (MW, MWh, CUR for the model currency, tonnes of CO2). FlexTool does not check units: a model may use others consistently. dimensionless is a ratio or a share; MW or count is a number of units when virtual_unitsize is above 0.
    • Default is the default of the schema. When FlexTool runs it may derive or default other values; the results show what the model used.
    • Allowed shapes says what a value may be: a constant, or a map (an indexed table) over periods, over time, or over both (one or two index levels); a stochastic-time map has three levels (branch, analysis_time, time). A map whose index is something else names it: a map: constraint name → coefficient. Where FlexTool checks the shape, this is what it accepts; otherwise it comes from the description or the value types of the schema (a map with n index levels).
    • The same table as an Excel data dictionary, with the parameters a database uses: python -m flextool.docs_build --xlsx catalogue.xlsx --input-db input_data.sqlite (see Make a data dictionary for your model).

    commodity

    A fuel or other product bought from, or sold to, outside the modelled system at a price (natural gas, coal, hydrogen for export).

    parameter unit allowed shapes default allowed values group definition
    co2_content tCO2/MWh a constant emission Constant.
    price CUR/MWh or other unit a constant, or a map over periods or over time (1 level) basics Price of the commodity. Constant, period or time.
    price_ladder_annual CUR/MWh; MWh/a a map over tiers and price / quantity (2 levels), or a map over periods, tiers and price / quantity (3 levels) basics Stepped supply curve for price_method price_ladder_annual. Map of tier → price and quantity (optionally per period). Quantity is per year; a shorter solve gets its share by the hours it covers. quantity = inf can be used to mark an unlimited last tier.
    price_ladder_cumulative CUR/MWh; MWh a map over tiers and price / quantity (2 levels) basics Stepped supply curve for price_method='price_ladder_cumulative'. 2d map with rows 'tier,price,quantity' — one row per tier, giving the tier's unit price and its cumulative quantity cap. 1-based integer tier index. quantity=inf marks an unbounded tail tier. Period-agnostic — the cap is a single total across the full model horizon.
    price_method a constant price price, price_ladder_annual, price_ladder_cumulative basics How the commodity's price enters the LP. 'price' = scalar or time-series price x flow (current behaviour); 'price_ladder_annual' = stepped supply curve with a per-year quantity cap per tier; 'price_ladder_cumulative' = stepped supply curve with a total-model quantity cap per tier (handoff-carried across rolling solves).
    unitsize MWh a constant basics Per-commodity scaling coefficient applied to the v_trade tier variable when the commodity uses the price-ladder feature (gated by commodity.price_method = price_ladder_annual or price_ladder_cumulative). When set, v_trade is expressed in user-MWh divided by this value; pick the unitsize so the largest tier quantity sits at O(10) in the scaled LP. The coefficient multiplies v_trade in the commodity_ladder_balance LHS, the per-tier cap LHS, and the per-tier objective term. When absent (the default), the price-ladder consumer substitutes 1.0 internally so v_trade is in user-MWh (identity scaling). Ignored entirely when commodity.price_method is not a price_ladder_* value.

    connection

    A link that moves energy between two nodes: a transmission line, an interconnector or a pipeline, with its capacity, losses and costs.

    parameter unit allowed shapes default allowed values group definition
    availability dimensionless a constant, or a map over periods or over time (1 level), or a map over periods and time (2 levels) 1 basics e.g. 0.9 means 90%. Fraction of capacity available for connection flows. Constant, period, time or period+time.
    constraint_capacity_coeff unit of the constraint per MW a map: constraint name → coefficient (1 level) constraint A map of coefficients (Index: constraint name, value: coefficient). Puts the connection's capacity in service in each period on the left side of the user constraint (existing plus investments still in service minus retirements). Coefficient per MW. Capacity in construction is not included.
    constraint_cumulative_pre_built_capacity_coeff unit of the constraint per MW a map: constraint name → coefficient (1 level) constraint A map of coefficients (Index: constraint name, value: coefficient). Puts the capacity in service at the start of each period on the left side of the user constraint. This is existing capacity plus capacity that came online in earlier periods, minus earlier retirements. Capacity coming online in the period itself is in the invested term. Coefficient per MW.
    constraint_in_construction_capacity_coeff unit of the constraint per MW a map: constraint name → coefficient (1 level) constraint A map of coefficients (Index: constraint name, value: coefficient). Puts the capacity in construction in each period on the left side of the user constraint. Capacity is in construction from the period of the investment decision until it comes online. It is zero without a construction lead time. Coefficient per MW.
    constraint_invested_capacity_coeff unit of the constraint per MW a map: constraint name → coefficient (1 level) constraint A map of coefficients (Index: constraint name, value: coefficient). Puts the capacity that comes online in each period on the left side of the user constraint. Without a construction lead time, this is the capacity invested in that period. Coefficient per MW.
    constraint_online_coeff unit of the constraint per unit online a map: constraint name → coefficient (1 level) constraint A map of coefficients (Index: constraint name, value: coefficient). Puts the number of units of the connection online in each time step on the left side of the user constraint. One unit is virtual_unitsize; without it, the largest existing capacity, else 1000 MW (set virtual_unitsize when the capacity is invested). Needs online variables (startup_method linear or binary); a coefficient without them is an error. With linear startup the number is continuous. For MW online, use the unit size (MW per unit) as the coefficient.
    constraint_retired_capacity_coeff unit of the constraint per MW a map: constraint name → coefficient (1 level) constraint A map of coefficients (Index: constraint name, value: coefficient). Puts the capacity retired in each period on the left side of the user constraint. Coefficient per MW.
    construction_lead_time years a constant, or a map over periods (1 level) investment Time from the investment decision until the capacity starts to operate. The start is rounded to the start of a period (see construction_lead_time_method). Investments whose rounded start is after the end of the solve or the model horizon are not offered, with a warning. Constant or period. Default 0.
    construction_lead_time_method a constant immediate, closest_seam, previous_seam, next_seam investment How the start of operation (decision year plus construction_lead_time) is rounded to the start of a period. immediate: no lead time, the capacity operates from the decision period. closest_seam: the nearest period start; a tie goes to the later one. previous_seam: the period start before. next_seam: the period start after. Without a value: immediate when there is no lead time, else closest_seam.
    cumulative_max_capacity MW a constant, or a map over periods (1 level) investment Maximum cumulative capacity (considers existing, invested and retired capacity). Constant or period.
    cumulative_min_capacity MW a constant, or a map over periods (1 level) investment Minimum cumulative capacity (considers existing, invested and retired capacity). Constant or period.
    delay hours a constant, or a map with 1 index level tech_advanced Not yet applied to connections (planned): a connection delay is ignored (the connection is modelled as if no delay were set) and the model warns about it. Model a delayed flow with a unit. Intended meaning: a time delay between the input node and the output node, either a constant time difference in hours or a map of time differences (index: time difference in hours, value: weight; the weights should sum to 1).
    discount_rate dimensionless a constant, or a map over periods (1 level) 0.05 investment e.g. 0.05 means 5% per year. Discount rate for investments (WACC). Reflects the financing cost and risk premium for this technology. When the model inflation_rate > 0, this should be a nominal rate. When inflation_rate = 0, this should be a real rate. Used to annualize investment costs over the lifetime: annuity = invest_cost x r / (1 - (1 + r)^-lifetime). 0 gives the straight-line annuity invest_cost / lifetime. No value means 0.05. Must be 0 or above. Constant or period.
    efficiency dimensionless a constant, or a map over periods or over time (1 level), or a map over periods and time (2 levels) 1 basics e.g. 0.9 means 90%. Efficiency of the connection. Constant, period, time or period+time.
    existing MW or count a constant, or a map over periods (1 level) basics Existing capacity or number of units. If virtual_unitsize is given, then existing is the number of units (capacity = existing × virtual_unitsize). Otherwise existing is the existing capacity of a single unit (typically in MW). Constant or period.
    fixed_cost CUR/kW/a a constant, or a map over periods (1 level) investment Annual fixed cost. New capacity pays it only while it operates. Constant or period.
    invest_cost CUR/kW a constant, or a map over periods (1 level) investment Overnight investment cost of new capacity. It is paid as an annuity over the years the capacity operates. Construction time costs with their interest must be included here. Constant or period: the value of the period when the capacity is built (comes online) is used.
    invest_max_period MW a constant, or a map over periods (1 level) investment Largest allowed capacity coming online in each period. Existing capacity and retirements do not change it. With a construction lead time, the investment is decided earlier. Constant or period.
    invest_max_total MW a constant investment Largest allowed total investment over all solves. Every investment decision counts, also reinvestments and investments that are no longer in service. Retirements and existing capacity do not reduce it. To limit the capacity in service, use cumulative_max_capacity. Constant.
    invest_method a constant not_allowed not_allowed, invest_no_limit, invest_period, invest_total, invest_period_total, retire_no_limit, retire_period, retire_total, retire_period_total, invest_retire_no_limit, invest_retire_period, invest_retire_total, invest_retire_period_total, cumulative_limits investment Choice of investment method: not_allowed, invest and retire indicate availability of investment and retirement. no_limit removes all limits even if there are limiting values set. period uses values set in invest/retire_max/min_period. total uses values set in invest/retire_max/min_total.
    invest_min_period MW a constant, or a map over periods (1 level) investment Smallest required capacity coming online in each period. Existing capacity and retirements do not change it. With a construction lead time, the investment is decided earlier. Constant or period.
    invest_min_total MW a constant investment Smallest required total investment over all solves. Every investment decision counts, also reinvestments and investments that are no longer in service. Retirements and existing capacity do not reduce it. To limit the capacity in service, use cumulative_max_capacity. Constant.
    is_DC a constant no yes, no network Whether the connection is DC. Its flow into a group with a non-synchronous limit counts as non-synchronous, in both directions. Default no.
    lifetime years a constant, or a map over periods (1 level) investment Operating lifetime. It starts when the capacity starts to operate: with a construction lead time, after the construction. Used with the interest rate to calculate the annuity. What happens at the end of the lifetime depends on lifetime_method: reinvest_automatic keeps the capacity to the end of the model horizon, reinvest_choice retires it, no_investment retires it once. Required when the connection invests or retires with a cost (invest_cost, salvage_value), and when lifetime_method is reinvest_choice or no_investment (also for existing capacity without investment). Constant or period.
    lifetime_method a constant reinvest_automatic reinvest_automatic, reinvest_choice, no_investment investment Choice how the investments behave after unit runs out of lifetime. Automatic reinvestment causes the model to keep the capacity until the end of model horizon and applies the annualized investment cost without further model choice. Choice of reinvestment removes the capacity at the end of the lifetime and the model needs to decide how much new capacity is to be built.
    other_operational_cost CUR/MWh a constant, or a map over periods or over time (1 level), or a map over periods and time (2 levels) basics Other operational variable cost for trasferring over the connection. Constant, period, time or period+time.
    reactance p.u. a constant network Per-unit reactance of the transmission line. Used for DC power flow when the connection is within a nodeGroup that has transfer_method set to dc_power_flow_with_angles. The susceptance used in the flow equation is base_MVA / reactance.
    retire_max_period MW a constant, or a map over periods (1 level) retirement Largest allowed retirement in each period. Counts only what is retired in that period; existing capacity and investments do not change it. Constant or period.
    retire_max_total MW a constant retirement Largest allowed total retirement over all solves. Investments and existing capacity do not change it. Constant.
    retire_min_period MW a constant, or a map over periods (1 level) retirement Smallest required retirement in each period. Counts only what is retired in that period; existing capacity and investments do not change it. Constant or period.
    retire_min_total MW a constant retirement Smallest required total retirement over all solves. Investments and existing capacity do not change it. Constant.
    salvage_value CUR/kW a constant, or a map over periods (1 level) retirement Salvage value for retiring capacity. Constant or period.
    startup_cost CUR/MW a constant, or a map over periods (1 level) tech_advanced Cost of starting up one MW of capacity. Constant or period.
    startup_method a constant no_startup no_startup, linear, binary tech_advanced Gives the connection online capacity in both directions. Flows and reserves are limited by the online capacity, and startup_cost is paid per MW brought online. No minimum load or minimum up/down time. Not allowed for dc_power_flow_with_angles lines.
    transfer_method a constant regular no_losses_no_variable_cost, regular, exact, variable_cost_only, unidirectional basics Choice of transfer method. Options: regular (default), no_losses_no_variable_cost, variable_cost_only, exact, unidirectional. 'unidirectional' restricts flow to source → sink only (single non-negative variable); 'regular'/'exact'/'variable_cost_only' are bidirectional two-variable variants; 'no_losses_no_variable_cost' is a single signed variable with no losses and no VOM.
    virtual_unitsize MW a constant basics Size of one unit. With it, existing is the number of units. Binary startups commit whole units of this size. Investments and retirements are continuous; their limits are in MW. No value: the existing capacity, or 1000 MW without it.

    constraint

    A constraint the user writes: its terms (flows, capacities, storage states, units online) come from the constraint_..._coeff parameters of the other classes; here its sense and its constant. A constraint with only capacity terms holds once per period, otherwise once per time step.

    parameter unit allowed shapes default allowed values group definition
    constant the constraint's own unit a constant, or a map over periods (1 level) 0 constraint Right-hand side of the user constraint. A period map gives each period its own value; it must cover every period of the solve. Constant or period.
    constraint_period_method a constant per_timestep per_timestep, period_total constraint How the user constraint is applied in time. per_timestep (default): a row for every time step (a constraint with only capacity terms has one row per period). period_total: one row per period over the sum of the time steps, each step's terms times its duration and weight, annualised (divided by the period's share of a year): flows count in MWh per year and units online in unit-hours per year; capacity terms and their existing capacity are multiplied by the hours of the year, and constant is per year. A state coefficient is not allowed. In a rolling solve the row is written only in a roll that realizes the whole period; otherwise the solve leaves it out with a warning. Constant.
    is_enabled a constant yes yes, no constraint Whether the entity is enabled. Set to 'no' to disable without deleting the entity. Constant.
    sense a constant equal greater_than, less_than, equal constraint The sense of the constraint ('greater_than', 'less_than' or 'equal').

    flowGroup

    A set of flows (a unit or a connection into or out of a node) that share limits, such as a maximum instant flow or a cumulative flow over a period.

    parameter unit allowed shapes default allowed values group definition
    flow_aggregator a constant none none, dispatch_plots_only, standalone_aggregator_only, both output Choice of flow-aggregation method for this flowGroup: 'none' (used for flow limits only, no aggregated output), 'dispatch_plots_only' (used as an aggregated flowGroup within 'print_dispatch' group of nodes - do not include one flow in multiple flow aggregators to avoid double counting), 'standalone_aggregator_only' (just make time series available for manual output processing / simple plots), 'both'.
    max_cumulative_flow MW a constant, or a map over periods (1 level) flow_limit Largest allowed average flow into the group. A constant gives one limit per solve; a period map gives one limit per period. Average flow times the weighted hours gives the energy limit. Constant or period.
    max_instant_flow MW a constant, or a map over periods or over time (1 level), or a map over periods and time (2 levels) flow_limit Maximum instantenous flow for the aggregated flow of all group members. Constant, period, time or period+time.
    min_cumulative_flow MW a constant, or a map over periods (1 level) flow_limit Smallest required average flow into the group. A constant gives one limit per solve; a period map gives one limit per period. Average flow times the weighted hours gives the energy limit. Constant or period.
    min_instant_flow MW a constant, or a map over periods or over time (1 level), or a map over periods and time (2 levels) flow_limit Minimum instantenous flow for the aggregated flow of all group members. Constant, period, time or period+time.

    group

    A set of units, connections and nodes that share limits or targets (investment limits, CO2 limits, capacity margin, inertia) or that are reported together.

    parameter unit allowed shapes default allowed values group definition
    base_MVA MVA a constant network Base power for the per-unit system used in DC power flow. Default 100. susceptance = base_MVA / reactance_pu.
    candidate_precapacity_to_avoid_big_m MW a constant network Small pre-existing capacity assigned to investment candidate connections in DC power flow groups that have zero existing capacity. This avoids the need for big-M / MIP constraints by ensuring the angle constraint is always active. Default 0.1 MW. The value should be small enough to not affect results but large enough for numerical stability.
    capacity_margin MW a constant, or a map over periods (1 level) investment Capacity the group needs above its demand at every time step of an investment period. Used only with capacity_margin_method manual. No value means 0 (the demand must still be covered). Constant or period.
    capacity_margin_method a constant none none, manual, share_of_peak investment Whether the group needs a capacity margin in investment solves, and how the margin is given. none (default): no margin. manual: capacity_margin MW. share_of_peak: capacity_margin_share times the group's peak demand in the period. At every time step of an investment period, the capacity of the group's firm units (capacity x availability x capacity_max_coeff) and VRE units (capacity x profile x availability) must cover the group's demand plus the margin. Units fed from a storage node, connections and the units consuming from the group count by their actual flow, so exports add to the requirement; connections and lossy units inside the group are left out (their losses are not required). Units fed from fuel or commodity nodes count at capacity: the fuel is assumed to last.
    capacity_margin_share dimensionless a constant, or a map over periods (1 level) investment e.g. 0.15 means 15%. Capacity margin as a share of the group's peak demand: the largest sum of the demand of the group's nodes over the period's full time series (before representative periods and new_stepduration). Used only with capacity_margin_method share_of_peak. No value means 0. Constant or period.
    co2_max_period tCO2/a a constant, or a map over periods (1 level) emission Largest annual CO2 emissions in each period. In a rolling solve, each roll may use the part of the limit its horizon reaches, minus what earlier rolls already emitted in that period. Constant or period.
    co2_max_total tCO2 a constant emission Largest CO2 emissions over the whole model horizon. Each period counts its annual emissions times its years_represented. Rolling solves and chains of solves pass the remaining budget on. Each stochastic forecast gets the whole remaining budget. Constant.
    co2_method a constant none none, price, period, total, price_period, price_total, period_total, price_period_total emission Choice of the CO2 method: none, price, period, total, price_period, price_total, period_total, price_period_total
    co2_price CUR/tCO2 a constant, or a map over periods or over time (1 level), or a map over periods and time (2 levels) emission CO2 price for a group of nodes. Constant, period, time or period+time.
    cumulative_max_capacity MW or MWh a constant, or a map over periods (1 level) investment Maximum cumulative capacity for a group of entities (considers existing, invested and retired capacity). Constant or period.
    cumulative_min_capacity MW or MWh a constant, or a map over periods (1 level) investment Minimum cumulative capacity for a group of entities (considers existing, invested and retired capacity). Constant or period.
    decomposition_method a constant none none, benders_regional solve_advanced Decomposition strategy to apply to this group. Currently supported: 'none' (no decomposition — default), 'benders_regional' (group is solved as an independent region in the Benders master/subproblem decomposition, with shared-commodity trade coupling handled by the master).
    has_inertia a constant no yes, no network Whether the group has an inertia constraint. Without yes, inertia_limit is ignored.
    has_non_synchronous a constant no yes, no network Whether the group has a non-synchronous share limit. Without yes, non_synchronous_limit is ignored.
    include_stochastics a constant no yes, no model Includes the stochastic branches to be used for the nodes/units/connections in this group
    inertia_limit MWs a constant, or a map over periods or over time (1 level), or a map over periods and time (2 levels) network Minimum synchronous inertia in the group. Used only when has_inertia is yes. No value (or 0) means no requirement. Constant, period, time or period+time. A time map must give a value for every time step of the solve (a missing time step is an error). Over a coarse time step the row uses the step mean.
    invest_max_period MW or MWh a constant, or a map over periods (1 level) investment Largest allowed capacity coming online in each period, summed over the group's units and connections (MW) and storage nodes (MWh). With a construction lead time, the investment is decided earlier. Constant or period.
    invest_max_total MW or MWh a constant investment Largest allowed total investment of the group over all solves. Every investment decision counts; retirements do not reduce it. Constant.
    invest_method a constant not_active not_active, invest_no_limit, invest_period, invest_total, invest_period_total, retire_no_limit, retire_period, retire_total, retire_period_total, invest_retire_no_limit, invest_retire_period, invest_retire_total, invest_retire_period_total, cumulative_limits investment How the group limits investment and retirement of its members. not_active: the group sets no limits. invest / retire / invest_retire: which decisions the limits apply to. _period uses the …_period limits, _total the …_total limits, _period_total both. no_limit: no limits even if values are set. cumulative_limits: uses the cumulative capacity limits.
    invest_min_period MW or MWh a constant, or a map over periods (1 level) investment Smallest required capacity coming online in each period, summed over the group's units and connections (MW) and storage nodes (MWh). With a construction lead time, the investment is decided earlier. Constant or period.
    invest_min_total MW or MWh a constant investment Smallest required total investment of the group over all solves. Every investment decision counts; retirements do not reduce it. Constant.
    new_stepduration hours a constant timeline Balances the energy of the group's nodes once per step of this length instead of every time step, e.g. daily hydrogen next to hourly electricity. Within the step, energy can move freely between time steps, and a storage node has one state per step. A unit or connection whose nodes are all in such groups also uses the longer step: it has one flow, units online and reserve per step, so the model gets smaller. Its limits, and rows that combine only such units and connections, apply to their average over the step, and ramp limits apply between steps. Units and connections with a node outside these groups keep the solve's time step, and so do those with a delay (an info line names them). Listing units or connections has no effect. Must be a multiple of the solve's step.
    non_synchronous_limit dimensionless a constant, or a map over periods or over time (1 level), or a map over periods and time (2 levels) network e.g. 0.8 means 80%. Largest share of non-synchronous supply in the group, measured against the real demand. Flows between the group's nodes do not count. Used only when has_non_synchronous is yes; no value means 0. Constant, period, time or period+time. A time map must give a value for every time step of the solve (a missing time step is an error). Over a coarse time step the row uses the step mean.
    penalty_capacity_margin CUR/kW/a a constant, or a map over periods (1 level) 5000 investment Penalty for violating the capacity margin constraint. Uses operational discounting (not annualized over lifetime like investment costs), so the value is not directly comparable to annualized investment costs. Constant or period.
    penalty_inertia CUR/MWs per hour a constant, or a map over periods (1 level) 5000 network Penalty for violating the inertia constraint. Cost scales with the duration of the violation. Constant or period.
    penalty_non_synchronous CUR/MWh a constant, or a map over periods (1 level) 5000 network Penalty for violating the non synchronous constraint. Constant or period.
    print_dispatch a constant yes, no output Combines unit/connection flows, flowGroup aggregated flows, and other balance elements (slack, demand, internal_losses, etc.) into a set of timeseries and a preconfigured plot per group.
    print_indicators a constant yes, no output Output indicators for the set of nodes in the group (Loss of Load, VRE generation, etc.).
    reference_node a constant network Name of the reference bus node (angle fixed to zero) for DC power flow. Optional — if not specified, automatically selected as the node with the largest existing capacity in each connected component of the DC power flow network.
    retire_max_period MW or MWh a constant, or a map over periods (1 level) retirement Largest allowed retirement in each period, summed over the group's members. Needs a retire …_period option in the group's invest_method. Constant or period.
    retire_max_total MW or MWh a constant retirement Largest allowed total retirement of the group over all solves. Needs a retire …_total option in the group's invest_method. Constant.
    retire_min_period MW or MWh a constant, or a map over periods (1 level) retirement Smallest required retirement in each period, summed over the group's members. Needs a retire …_period option in the group's invest_method. Constant or period.
    retire_min_total MW or MWh a constant retirement Smallest required total retirement of the group over all solves. Needs a retire …_total option in the group's invest_method. Constant.
    share_loss_of_load a constant no equal, inflow_weighted, no flow_limit Force the upward slack of the nodes in this group to be equal or inflow (demand) weighted
    transfer_method a constant use_connection_transfer_methods use_connection_transfer_methods, no_losses_no_variable_cost, regular, exact, variable_cost_only, dc_power_flow_with_angles, unidirectional network Override transfer_method for all connections within this nodeGroup. Options: use_connection_transfer_methods (default, no override), no_losses_no_variable_cost, regular, exact, variable_cost_only, unidirectional, dc_power_flow_with_angles. Setting 'unidirectional' gates every member connection to one-way flow (source → sink only). Setting 'dc_power_flow_with_angles' uses B-theta DC power flow (requires reactance on connections).

    cumulative_min_capacity MW a constant, or a map over periods (1 level) investment Minimum cumulative capacity (considers existing, invested and retired capacity). Constant or period.

Must be 0 or above. Constant or period.
    efficiency dimensionless a constant, or a map over periods or over time (1 level), or a map over periods and time (2 levels), or a stochastic-time map (branch, analysis_time, time; 3 levels) 1 basics Efficiency of the unit at full load: outputs / inputs, each flow weighted by its conversion_flow_coeff (default 1, giving plain sum of outputs / sum of inputs). May be above 1: a heat pump with COP 3 has 3; a unit whose output is measured in another unit of measure (e.g. m³ of water per MWh of electricity) uses that ratio. A min_load_efficiency unit can have different efficiency below full load (see efficiency_at_min_load). No effect on a unit without inputs or without outputs. Constant, period, time, period+time or stochastic time.
    efficiency_at_min_load dimensionless a constant, or a map over periods or over time (1 level), or a map over periods and time (2 levels) basics Output / input at minimum load, like efficiency (e.g. 0.4; may be above 1). Efficiency of a min_load_efficiency unit at its minimum load. Fuel use is a straight line from minimum load (this efficiency) to full load (efficiency). Has no effect when min_load is 0: the efficiency at zero output is not defined. No value: efficiency applies at minimum load too. Constant, period, time or period+time.
    existing MW or count a constant, or a map over periods (1 level) basics Existing capacity or number of units. If virtual_unitsize is given, then existing is the number of units (capacity = existing × virtual_unitsize). Otherwise existing is the existing capacity of a single unit (typically in MW).     invest_cost CUR/kW a constant, or a map over periods (1 level) investment Overnight investment cost of new capacity. It is paid as an annuity over the years the capacity operates.     invest_max_period MW a constant, or a map over periods (1 level) investment Largest allowed capacity coming online in each period. Existing capacity and retirements do not change it. With a construction lead time, the investment is decided earlier. Constant or period.
    invest_max_total MW a constant investment Largest allowed total investment over all solves. Every investment decision counts, also reinvestments and investments that are no longer in service.     invest_method a constant not_allowed not_allowed, invest_no_limit, invest_period, invest_total, invest_period_total, retire_no_limit, retire_period, retire_total, retire_period_total, invest_retire_no_limit, invest_retire_period, invest_retire_total, invest_retire_period_total, cumulative_limits investment Choice of investment method: not_allowed, invest and retire indicate availability of investment and retirement. no_limit removes all limits even if there are limiting values set. period uses values set in invest/retire_max/min_period. total uses values set in invest/retire_max/min_total.
    invest_min_period MW a constant, or a map over periods (1 level) investment Smallest required capacity coming online in each period. Existing capacity and retirements do not change it. With a construction lead time, the investment is decided earlier. Constant or period.
    invest_min_total MW a constant investment Smallest required total investment over all solves. Every investment decision counts, also reinvestments and investments that are no longer in service. Retirements and existing capacity do not reduce it. To limit the capacity in service, use cumulative_max_capacity. Constant.
    lifetime years a constant, or a map over periods (1 level) investment Operating lifetime. It starts when the capacity starts to operate: with a construction lead time, after the construction. Used with the interest rate to calculate the annuity. What happens at the end of the lifetime depends on lifetime_method: reinvest_automatic keeps the capacity to the end of the model horizon, reinvest_choice retires it, no_investment retires it once. Required when the unit invests or retires with a cost (invest_cost, salvage_value), and when lifetime_method is reinvest_choice or no_investment (also for existing capacity without investment).     lifetime_method a constant reinvest_automatic reinvest_automatic, reinvest_choice, no_investment investment Choice how the investments behave after unit runs out of lifetime. Automatic reinvestment causes the model to keep the capacity until the end of model horizon and applies the annualized investment cost without further model choice.     min_load dimensionless a constant, or a map over periods or over time (1 level), or a map over periods and time (2 levels), or a stochastic-time map (branch, analysis_time, time; 3 levels) basics 0-1, share of the online capacity.     retire_min_total MW a constant retirement Smallest required total retirement over all solves. Investments and existing capacity do not change it. Constant.

    startup_cost CUR/MW a constant, or a map over periods (1 level) tech_advanced Cost of starting up one MW of capacity. Constant or period.
